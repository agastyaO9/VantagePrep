The Problem

Little Classes. Big Progress.

Most tutoring programs pack in as many students as possible. Parents pay, drop their kids off, and hope for the best — with no real way to know what's actually happening in the room.

🕳️

The "Black Box" Problem

You send your child to class and trust that learning happens. But you have no visibility into how they're doing, what they understand, or what they're struggling with.

🙈

Lost in the Crowd

In a room of 20–30 kids, quiet struggles go unnoticed. Students fall behind silently and often finish a course having learned almost nothing.

📉

No Real Accountability

Without regular testing or pressure to perform, there's no way to know if a student actually absorbed the material — or just sat through it.

1
This is uncommon — and that's the point

Our Approach

5–10 students. One instructor. Total transparency. No exceptions.

This is the core of everything ATOI does. While most programs maximize headcount, ATOI intentionally caps every class at 5–10 students so that every single child gets real, individual, working attention — not a lecture they sit through and forget.
